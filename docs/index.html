Marshy's Musings

A voice recorder for the M5StickS3 and its Android companion app, side by side. A simulation: every button works, nothing is recorded.

firmware 2.14.0app 0.16.0

The stick

Drawn to scale. Settings on the stick has seven rows, including Phone (paired, or forget it). Sideways: KEY1 (the blue key) records. KEY2 (the edge button) shows the files; held, it opens Settings: seven themes including Tape, brightness, Cat Slots, Dice, web mode. Turn it upright for the tag picker, or the pay table while playing slots.

The phone

The first Sync asks to pair with the stick; after that the phone syncs on its own whenever the stick has new notes. Sync pulls what's on the stick. Each note is transcribed, then tidied by the on-device AI (Gemini Nano) with a prompt you can edit. Tap a note to compare the tidied and spoken versions or tidy it again.

Watching for the stick
Stick

Tag, then talk

Upright, the picker chooses Work, Run or Intention and a sub-tag (sticky across power cycles), and the format: Opus 16 kHz by default, WAV when a file must play anywhere. Sideways, KEY1 starts and stops. Files are run-thought_0042.opus, numbered in the order spoken.

Stick

Advertise, then sleep

Bluetooth advertises whenever the stick is on and not recording. Screen dims at 1 minute, off at 2; power off at 10 idle minutes (deep sleep on USB, KEY1 wakes). Only a real file transfer counts as activity.

Phone

Pair once, then wake and pull

The stick has an id and remembers one phone; a new phone asks once, and taking over needs KEY1 on the stick. The stick advertises a "new notes" flag, and the paired phone's radio wakes the app only when it is set. Each file is streamed, checked for size, written, read back, and only then deleted from the stick. The app never deletes what it does not hold.

Phone

Transcribe

whisper.cpp on the phone, in ~30-second pieces cut at quiet moments, as a foreground job. Each finished piece is saved, so a stop resumes where it left off.

Phone

Tidy, file, list

Gemini Nano rewrites the note as bullets with your editable prompt (the spoken text is kept). Entries go into musings.md and per-category journals, mirrored to a Drive folder. "Add to list, …" becomes a checkbox in musings-list.md.

Phone → stick

Update over the air

The app carries the matching firmware. Settings → Firmware → Update sends it over Bluetooth into the stick's spare slot; the stick checks the CRC, restarts into it, and falls back if it fails to boot.

Power, in round numbers

The ESP32-S3, mic, accelerometer and radio draw roughly 60–80 mA while on, so a 250 mAh charge is a few hours of being switched on. A ring like the Pebble Index sleeps at microamps between presses. A "sleep when idle" mode that copies that is the next candidate.

This page is a browser simulation of firmware 2.14.0 and app 0.16.0. Notes, transcripts and places in it are examples. Transcription (whisper.cpp) and tidy-up (Gemini Nano) run on the phone in the real app; here they are stand-ins. Tango the cat is the author's own pixel art.
